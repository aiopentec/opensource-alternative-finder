🗺️ Migration Steps

1
Audit and Clean Up Your Miro Boards
Before initiating any migration, review your active Miro boards to archive outdated content and consolidate active projects. Because Excalidraw relies on a simpler organizational structure without Miro's nested folders and infinite frames hierarchy, slimming down your data upfront prevents clutter.
2
Export Content from Miro
Open your crucial Miro boards and export essential visual assets. While Miro does not offer a direct native one-click export into Excalidraw's unique JSON format, you can export high-resolution vector SVGs or PNGs of your key diagrams to use as static reference layers in your new Excalidraw workspaces.
3
Set Up Your Excalidraw Workspace
Navigate to Excalidraw and decide whether you will use the free web version, self-host via open-source code for total data privacy, or adopt Excalidraw+ for team collaboration features. Configure your team organization settings and invite members via shareable workspace links so everyone is ready to receive the new boards.
4
Recreate Core Diagrams and Build Templates
Manually rebuild your essential team templates—like retrospective grids, architecture flows, and user journey maps—directly inside Excalidraw using its library feature. Save these custom assets to your team library so members can drag and drop them instantly, replicating Miro's template efficiency.
5
Onboard the Team to the Excalidraw UI
Host a short workshop to walk your team through Excalidraw's keyboard shortcuts, hand-drawn styling toggles, and laser-pointer collaboration tools. Emphasize that while Excalidraw lacks Miro's complex voting widgets and timer plugins, its raw speed and lack of learning curve will accelerate daily brainstorming.
6
Complete the Cutover and Cancel Miro Subscriptions
Transition your daily async workflows, meeting links, and documentation bookmarks from Miro to your new Excalidraw rooms. Once all active projects have been migrated or archived, downgrade or cancel your Miro paid subscription to immediately start capturing software cost savings.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I automatically convert my Miro boards directly into Excalidraw files?
No, there is currently no direct automated converter tool due to the structural differences between Miro's proprietary database and Excalidraw's JSON schema. You will need to export critical reference diagrams as SVGs or images and manually recreate active working workflows in Excalidraw.
Is Excalidraw really free compared to Miro's expensive pricing tiers?
Yes, the core Excalidraw web application is completely free and open-source with no user caps or paywalls on basic features. For teams requiring cloud storage, encrypted workspace sharing, and team management, Excalidraw offers a paid plus tier, but it remains significantly cheaper than Miro.
How does real-time collaboration in Excalidraw compare to Miro?
Excalidraw supports seamless real-time multi-user collaboration via shared room links, allowing teams to draw, edit, and follow each other's cursors simultaneously just like in Miro. However, it operates with a much lighter footprint, resulting in faster load times and zero lag even on lower-spec hardware.

📦 Migration Guide

How to Migrate from Monday.com to Plane

💰 Save $9-$19/user/month/month 🔓 Own your data 🖥️ Setup: Easy (~30 mins) 📅 August 31, 2026

Migrating from Monday.com to Plane is a strategic move for engineering and product teams looking to escape escalating SaaS subscription fees while gaining deeper control over their workflow data. While Monday.com excels as a flexible, colorful spreadsheet-like tracker with per-user costs ranging from $9 to $19 per month, its generalized approach can become cluttered and expensive for software-focused teams. Plane offers a powerful, open-source alternative designed specifically for issue tracking, cycles, and product roadmapping without the punishing per-seat pricing.

Teams typically make this switch when their Monday.com boards swell with complex automation recipes, custom columns, and sub-items, yet lack the native developer-centric primitives like git integrations, cycle planning, and robust markdown documentation found in Plane. Moving to Plane means transitioning from a tabular database mentality to a structured, cycle-driven development workflow. Whether you choose to self-host Plane for maximum data privacy or use their cloud tier, this migration guide will help you systematically transition your projects, historical data, and team habits from Monday.com's visual boards to Plane's purpose-built project management interface.

🗺️ Migration Steps

1
Export Data from Monday.com
Navigate to your Monday.com boards and export your item lists, status columns, update logs, and owner details into CSV or Excel format. Because Monday.com relies heavily on complex column types like formulas, dropdowns, and mirror columns, you will need to flatten these data structures into standard text and status fields to ensure a clean import into Plane.
2
Configure Your Plane Workspace
Set up your organization within Plane by creating a new workspace and defining your project spaces to mirror your Monday.com board structure. Configure your custom states, priorities, and labels in Plane settings ahead of time so they match or logically map to the status columns you exported from Monday.com.
3
Map and Import Data
Use Plane native import tools or custom scripts via the Plane API to ingest your exported CSV files. Carefully map Monday.com item names to Plane issue titles, descriptions to issue bodies, and assignees to your newly invited Plane workspace users to preserve ownership history.
4
Rebuild Automations and Integrations
Recreate your essential Monday.com automation recipes—such as status-triggered notifications or automated assignees—using Plane native workflow rules and webhook integrations. Connect your GitHub or GitLab repositories to Plane now so developers can link pull requests directly to the newly migrated issues.
5
Team Onboarding and Training
Conduct a workshop to familiarize your team with Plane's keyboard shortcuts, cycle-based planning views, and modular issue layouts. Emphasize how Plane replaces Monday.com's tabular view with focused modules, pages, and cycles tailored for modern product development.
6
Perform the Final Cutover
Run a final delta export from Monday.com to capture any last-minute task updates made during the transition period. Once imported, lock your Monday.com boards to read-only mode, announce the official switch to your team, and begin managing all active sprints entirely within Plane.

⚠️ Common Challenges & How to Avoid Them

Monday.com's highly customized column types, mirror columns, and formula fields do not have direct 1:1 equivalents in Plane's data schema.
Flatten mirror and formula columns into static text or description notes in your CSV export before importing them into Plane's custom fields.
Team members accustomed to Monday.com's colorful, spreadsheet-like interface may initially struggle with Plane's developer-focused UI and cycle paradigms.
Host a dedicated training session highlighting Plane's view filters and keyboard navigation to demonstrate how it actually speeds up daily task updates compared to Monday.com.
Loss of historical Monday.com update logs, conversation threads, and item sub-items during a standard CSV migration.
Export Monday.com item updates as a separate text file and append them to the bottom of the corresponding issue description in Plane to preserve historical context.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I automatically sync Monday.com and Plane during a phased migration?
While native real-time sync does not exist, you can use webhook middleware or integration platforms like Zapier to forward newly created Monday.com items directly into Plane during a short overlap period.
How does self-hosting Plane compare to paying for Monday.com enterprise tiers?
Self-hosting Plane is free and open-source, requiring only your own cloud infrastructure costs, whereas Monday.com charges upwards of $16 to $19 per user per month for advanced security and automation features.
What happens to file attachments stored in Monday.com items during the migration?
Monday.com file attachments are linked via secure URLs that expire or become inaccessible outside the platform. You should download critical attachments locally and re-upload them directly to the corresponding Plane issues.

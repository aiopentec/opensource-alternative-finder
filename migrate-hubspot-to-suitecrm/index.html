How to Migrate from HubSpot to SuiteCRM

💰 Save Free / $15-$800+/month/month 🔓 Own your data 🖥️ Setup: Moderate (~1-2 hours) 📅 August 30, 2026

Migrating from HubSpot to SuiteCRM is a strategic move for businesses looking to eliminate escalating monthly SaaS subscription fees in favor of a completely open-source, self-hosted customer relationship management solution. While HubSpot offers an intuitive, polished cloud ecosystem, its pricing tiers scale aggressively as contact databases grow and advanced automation features are unlocked. Organizations often reach a tipping point where the recurring cost of HubSpot outweighs the utility of its proprietary platform.

SuiteCRM provides a powerful, highly customizable alternative that gives businesses total ownership of their data without per-user licensing penalties. Moving away from HubSpot means transitioning from a managed multi-tenant cloud to an infrastructure you control, whether deployed on-premise or via a private cloud. This migration allows companies to tailor every workflow, module, and database relation to their exact operational needs, bypassing the rigid framework limitations often encountered in HubSpot's Sales and Marketing Hubs.

🗺️ Migration Steps

1
Audit and Cleanse HubSpot Data
Before exporting, review your HubSpot CRM to remove duplicate contacts, obsolete deals, and unassigned company records. Standardize custom properties and picklists so they map cleanly to SuiteCRM fields later, ensuring a seamless data transition.
2
Export Data from HubSpot
Export your core CRM data from HubSpot using standard CSV format downloads for contacts, companies, deals, tickets, and notes. Ensure that unique identifiers and association IDs are preserved in your export files to maintain relationships between records during import.
3
Deploy and Configure SuiteCRM
Set up your SuiteCRM instance on your chosen server environment and configure global settings, currency, and user permissions. Recreate any custom modules, fields, and dropdown lists that match your previous HubSpot property structure.
4
Map and Import Data into SuiteCRM
Utilize SuiteCRM's built-in Import Wizard to upload your CSV files, carefully mapping HubSpot columns to corresponding SuiteCRM fields. Run a small test import first with a handful of contacts to verify that data integrity and parent-child relationships remain intact.
5
Rebuild Workflows and Integrations
Reconfigure your automated marketing sequences, deal stage triggers, and task assignments using SuiteCRM's Workflow Management tools. Reconnect essential business tools like email clients, telephony systems, and accounting software via custom webhooks or native SuiteCRM extensions.
6
Execute Team Onboarding and Final Cutover
Conduct comprehensive training sessions for your sales and support teams to familiarize them with SuiteCRM's layout and navigation. Perform a final delta export from HubSpot to capture any last-minute records, shut off HubSpot write-access, and officially launch SuiteCRM for daily operations.

⚠️ Common Challenges & How to Avoid Them

HubSpot's proprietary marketing automation logic and email templates do not automatically transfer to SuiteCRM.
Rebuild your email campaigns and triggers manually using SuiteCRM's built-in Campaign module and Workflow engine, or integrate an external email service provider like Mautic or SendGrid.
Maintaining complex parent-child associations and timeline notes during CSV imports can result in orphaned data.
Use SuiteCRM's specific import mapping rules for related records and run iterative test imports on subset batches before committing the full database.
Users accustomed to HubSpot's polished, minimal interface may experience friction adopting SuiteCRM's more traditional UI.
Customize the SuiteCRM dashboard and theme layouts to mirror familiar workflows, and provide dedicated role-based training before the final cutover.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I automatically migrate HubSpot workflows and email sequences to SuiteCRM?
No, automated workflows, email sequences, and marketing templates cannot be directly transferred due to structural differences between the platforms. You will need to recreate these automation rules using SuiteCRM's Workflow and Campaign modules after your data import is complete.
How are custom properties in HubSpot handled during a SuiteCRM migration?
You must manually recreate any custom properties and dropdown fields in SuiteCRM's Studio before initiating your CSV data import. Once these fields exist in SuiteCRM, you can map your exported HubSpot columns directly to them through the Import Wizard.
What happens to historical email logs and activity timelines when moving from HubSpot?
Historical notes, emails, and call logs can be exported from HubSpot as associated activity files, but they often require careful re-linking to their respective contact and deal records. You should structure your CSV exports to include parent record IDs so SuiteCRM can correctly attach historical logs during the import process.

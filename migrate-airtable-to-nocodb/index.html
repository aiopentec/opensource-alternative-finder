How to Migrate from Airtable to NocoDB

💰 Save Free / $10-$20/user/month/month 🔓 Own your data 🖥️ Setup: Very Easy (~5 mins) 📅 August 30, 2026

Migrating from Airtable to NocoDB is a strategic move for teams looking to break free from rigid cloud vendor lock-in, escalating per-user pricing tiers, and strict data governance limitations. While Airtable remains a gold standard for polished user interfaces and collaborative spreadsheet-database hybrids, its costs scale aggressively as teams grow and data volume increases. NocoDB offers a compelling open-source alternative that transforms any SQL database into a smart spreadsheet, retaining the relational data capabilities and views you rely on while giving you complete ownership of your infrastructure.

Organizations typically trigger this migration when they hit Airtable record limits on paid plans, require strict on-premise data residency for compliance, or want to connect directly to existing PostgreSQL, MySQL, or SQLite databases without synchronization middleware. By moving to NocoDB, you eliminate monthly seat fees and gain the flexibility to self-host via Docker, scale database performance independently, and integrate directly with custom backend services using native REST and GraphQL APIs.

🗺️ Migration Steps

1
Audit and Clean Your Airtable Bases
Review your existing Airtable workspace to identify unused bases, obsolete views, and redundant attachments that will bloat your migration. Clean up complex formula fields and dependent automations, documenting their logic so you can accurately recreate them in NocoDB.
2
Export Data from Airtable
Export your critical Airtable tables into CSV format individually, ensuring linked record fields are exported with primary key identifiers intact. For large bases, utilize Airtable's API or third-party scripting tools to extract JSON data and preserve exact relational structures.
3
Provision Your NocoDB Environment
Deploy NocoDB using Docker or your preferred cloud infrastructure, connecting it to your target database instance such as PostgreSQL or MySQL. Ensure your database storage and environment variables are properly configured to handle the incoming data volume and user concurrency.
4
Import Data and Rebuild Relations
Create new projects within NocoDB and use the CSV import utility to populate your tables. Manually re-establish link-to-another-record relationships and lookup fields, verifying that foreign keys map correctly to maintain data integrity across your relational schemas.
5
Configure Views, Automations, and Permissions
Recreate your essential grid, kanban, gallery, and calendar views within NocoDB to match your team's daily workflows. Set up role-based access controls and build webhook-driven automations to replace the native scripts and notifications previously managed in Airtable.
6
Perform Final Cutover and Team Onboarding
Run a final delta data sync from Airtable to capture any last-minute entries before officially cutting over production traffic. Conduct a training session for your team to familiarize them with NocoDB interface nuances, bookmark the new self-hosted URL, and decommission the old Airtable base.

⚠️ Common Challenges & How to Avoid Them

Airtable attachments and uploaded files do not transfer automatically via standard CSV exports.
Download all attachments locally using a bulk downloader script, host them in an S3-compatible bucket, and update your NocoDB attachment fields with the new public URLs.
Complex Airtable formulas using proprietary functions fail when pasted directly into NocoDB SQL-backed columns.
Rewrite your formulas using standard SQL syntax or native NocoDB formula builders, testing each calculated field thoroughly before team rollout.
Airtable's native workspace automations and scripting blocks do not have a direct one-click importer into NocoDB.
Translate your essential automation logic into NocoDB's webhook integrations or use Node.js scripts running on an external service like n8n to replicate the workflow triggers.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I import Airtable bases directly into NocoDB with a single click?
NocoDB does not currently offer a direct one-click API importer for entire Airtable workspaces due to proprietary schema differences. You will need to export your tables to CSV format and import them table by table while manually rebuilding complex relations and views.
How does NocoDB pricing compare to Airtable for large teams?
Airtable charges steep per-user monthly fees that scale rapidly as your organization adds more collaborators. NocoDB is free and open-source for self-hosting, meaning your costs are limited only by your underlying database and server infrastructure regardless of how many users you add.
What databases can NocoDB use compared to Airtable's closed backend?
While Airtable stores all your data on their proprietary cloud infrastructure, NocoDB connects directly to popular relational databases including PostgreSQL, MySQL, Microsoft SQL Server, SQLite, and Amazon Aurora, giving you total ownership and direct query access to your data.

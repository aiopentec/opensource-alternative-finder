HubSpot vs SuiteCRM (2026)

Overview

HubSpot is a cloud‑based inbound marketing, sales, and service platform that bundles CRM, email automation, and analytics. SuiteCRM is an open‑source, self‑hosted CRM that extends core sales and support functions through modules and custom code. This comparison helps marketers, sales ops, and IT leaders decide whether to stay on HubSpot’s paid SaaS model or migrate to a free, self‑hosted alternative. Switch if you need full data ownership, lower per‑seat cost at scale, or tighter integration with on‑premise systems.

Key Differences

  • Cost: HubSpot charges $15‑$800+ per month per user; SuiteCRM is free software (only server and support costs).
  • Data ownership: HubSpot stores data in its cloud with limited export; SuiteCRM gives you complete control of the database on your own servers.
  • Setup complexity: HubSpot is ready‑to‑use out of the box; SuiteCRM requires installation, configuration, and ongoing maintenance.
  • Scalability: HubSpot’s pricing rises sharply after 10‑15 users; SuiteCRM scales linearly with hardware, making 200+ users inexpensive.
  • Feature/Integration: HubSpot includes native email

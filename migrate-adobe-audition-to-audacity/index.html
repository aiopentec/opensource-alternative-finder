📦 Migration Guide

How to Migrate from Adobe Audition to Audacity

💰 Save $20.99-$54.99/month/month 🔓 Own your data 📅 August 28, 2026

Migrating from Adobe Audition to Audacity is a pragmatic move for creators, podcasters, and editors looking to eliminate recurring subscription costs. While Audition commands between $20.99 and $54.99 per month as part of the Creative Cloud ecosystem, Audacity remains completely free and open-source. For solo operators, hobbyists, or budget-conscious studios, dropping this monthly overhead provides immediate financial relief without sacrificing fundamental audio editing capabilities.

However, moving away from Audition means transitioning from a non-destructive, multitrack powerhouse with deep VST3/VST support to a simpler paradigm. Audacity has historically relied on destructive editing for single tracks, though it now features non-destructive clip handling and real-time effects in recent versions. Understanding how to map your workflow from Adobe's advanced spectral analysis and mixing desk to Audacity's interface will ensure a smooth transition and keep your production timeline on track.

🗺️ Migration Steps

1
Export Session Assets from Audition
Open your active Adobe Audition multitrack sessions and consolidate your audio files. Use the Export > Multitrack Mixdown or save individual stems as uncompressed WAV files to ensure zero quality loss before bringing them into Audacity.
2
Install and Configure Audacity
Download the latest version of Audacity from the official site. Install the FFmpeg library extension immediately so Audacity can natively open and export proprietary formats like AAC, AC3, and M4A that you previously handled in Audition.
3
Import Stems and Rebuild Multitrack Projects
Launch Audacity and drag your exported WAV stems into the timeline. Because Audition session files cannot be opened directly by Audacity, you will need to manually align your tracks and set up your project tempo and sample rates.
4
Map Essential Effects and Plugins
Scan your third-party VST plugins within Audacity to retain your favorite mastering tools. Replace built-in Audition staples, like the Parametric Equalizer and De-Esser, with Audacity's equivalent Effect menu options or equivalent free open-source plugins.
5
Train Your Team on the New Interface
Run a brief workshop to familiarize editors with Audacity's distinct keyboard shortcuts and toolbar layouts. Ensure everyone understands how to use the new real-time effects rack and clip manipulation tools that mimic Audition's non-destructive workflow.
6
Execute Final Cutover and Archive
Cancel your Adobe Audition subscription only after archiving all legacy .sesx project files for future reference. Move all new podcast episodes, voiceovers, and sound design projects entirely into Audacity for your day-to-day production.

⚠️ Common Challenges & How to Avoid Them

Audacity cannot open Adobe Audition .sesx multitrack session files directly.
Export all tracks as individual WAV stems starting from zero on the timeline in Audition, then import those stems into an empty Audacity project.
Audition's advanced spectral frequency display and healing brush tools are missing in their native form.
Use Audacity's Spectrogram view to locate problem frequencies and apply the Notch Filter or Noise Reduction effect to clean up audio artifacts.
Historically, Audacity was strictly destructive, making seasoned Audition users nervous about permanent edits.
Update to the latest version of Audacity, which now supports non-destructive clip movement, trimming, and real-time effects racks much like Audition.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can Audacity open my old Adobe Audition session files?
No, Audacity cannot read .sesx files because they are proprietary to Adobe. You must export your multitrack projects as individual audio stems from Audition before you can edit them in Audacity.
Is Audacity really free compared to Adobe Audition?
Yes, Audacity is 100% free and open-source software with no hidden fees or subscription tiers. Migrating saves you between $250 and $650 annually depending on your current Adobe Creative Cloud plan.
Can I use my VST plugins from Adobe Audition in Audacity?
Yes, Audacity supports VST and AU plugins on Windows and macOS. You can scan your existing plugin directories during setup to use many of the same mastering and restoration tools you used in Audition.

📦 Migration Guide

How to Migrate from Notion to AppFlowy

💰 Save $8-$20/user/month 🔓 Own your data 🖥️ Setup: Very Easy (~5 mins) 📅 September 06, 2026

Migrating from Notion to AppFlowy is an increasingly popular move for users seeking to escape rising subscription costs and cloud-only lock-in. While Notion is a powerful all-in-one workspace charging between eight and twenty dollars per user monthly, AppFlowy offers a compelling open-source alternative built with Rust and Flutter. Individuals and teams choose AppFlowy when they want complete ownership of their data, local-first performance, and the peace of mind that comes with zero proprietary cloud dependencies.

However, moving from a mature cloud ecosystem like Notion to a local-first open-source workspace requires deliberate planning. Notion stores pages in a proprietary cloud database format with deeply nested relational properties, whereas AppFlowy emphasizes local file management and modular architecture. This migration guide outlines the exact process to transition your databases, documents, and team workflows to AppFlowy without losing critical project history or organizational structure.

🗺️ Migration Steps

1
Export Your Notion Workspace
Open your Notion settings and navigate to Workspace Settings, then Export All Workspace Content. Choose Markdown and CSV format to ensure maximum compatibility for tables and text blocks. Download the resulting zip file once Notion finishes generating your data export package.
2
Install and Configure AppFlowy
Download the latest version of AppFlowy from the official website or GitHub repository for your operating system. Launch the desktop application and configure your local storage directory where your workspace files and SQLite databases will reside securely on your machine.
3
Import Markdown Files and Databases
Use AppFlowy import utilities to ingest your unzipped Notion Markdown files into your new local workspace folder. Recreate your Notion database structures manually using AppFlowy grid and board views, as direct automated schema mapping for complex relations is still evolving.
4
Rebuild Relations and Formulas
Review your imported databases to re-establish linked records and select properties that may have flattened during the markdown conversion. AppFlowy supports customizable database views, so map your Notion board, calendar, and table views to their corresponding AppFlowy layout equivalents.
5
Onboard Your Team and Set Up Sync
Introduce team members to the AppFlowy interface, emphasizing its speed and local-first data privacy advantages. Configure self-hosted synchronization or shared folder environments if your team requires collaborative access across multiple local machines.
6
Perform the Final Cutover
Verify that all critical project trackers, meeting notes, and knowledge base documents are fully functional in AppFlowy. Cancel your paid Notion subscription to immediately start capturing cost savings once your team has fully transitioned their daily workflows.

⚠️ Common Challenges & How to Avoid Them

Complex Notion relational databases and formula properties do not automatically map over during markdown import.
Rebuild key relations manually in AppFlowy using its native database views, and recreate essential formulas using AppFlowy's growing expression builder.
Notion's cloud-native real-time collaboration does not work out of the box in a local-first AppFlowy setup.
Set up AppFlowy Cloud or configure self-hosted synchronization infrastructure to enable multi-user real-time editing for your team.
Deeply nested page hierarchies and inline comments can get lost or flattened when exporting from Notion.
Organize your exported markdown folders carefully before importing, and manually reconstruct crucial nested page structures within AppFlowy's sidebar.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I import my Notion pages directly into AppFlowy with one click?
Not entirely. While AppFlowy supports importing Markdown and CSV files exported from Notion, complex database relations, inline comments, and specific page layouts require manual reorganization after the initial file import.
Is AppFlowy completely free compared to Notion?
Yes, AppFlowy is open-source software that you can download and use for free on your local machine. Unlike Notion, which charges tiered monthly subscription fees per user, AppFlowy gives you free control over your software and data.
How does AppFlowy handle team collaboration without Notion's cloud servers?
AppFlowy offers AppFlowy Cloud as an optional service for seamless synchronization, or you can choose to self-host your data infrastructure. This gives teams the flexibility to collaborate securely without relying on third-party proprietary servers.

📦 Migration Guide

How to Migrate from Salesforce to EspoCRM

💰 Save $25-$300+/user/month/month 🔓 Own your data 📅 August 26, 2026

Migrating from Salesforce to EspoCRM is a strategic move for organizations looking to escape skyrocketing per-user licensing fees while retaining robust CRM functionality. While Salesforce offers an extensive ecosystem, its steep subscription costs starting at $25 and scaling past $300 per user per month can quickly drain the budgets of small-to-midsize businesses. EspoCRM provides a compelling, cost-effective alternative as an open-source solution that can be self-hosted or deployed on affordable cloud infrastructure.

Organizations typically make this switch when they find themselves paying for advanced Salesforce features they never use, or when custom configurations become overly complex and expensive to maintain. EspoCRM offers a lightweight, intuitive interface, strong customization capabilities, and ownership of your data without vendor lock-in. By moving to EspoCRM, companies drastically reduce their software overhead while maintaining core capabilities like contact management, sales automation, and customer support tracking.

🗺️ Migration Steps

1
Audit and Export Salesforce Data
Begin by auditing your current Salesforce org to identify which standard and custom objects, fields, and attachments you actually need. Use tools like Salesforce Data Loader or native CSV export reports to extract accounts, contacts, opportunities, and activities. Clean your data during this phase to avoid importing legacy errors into your new EspoCRM environment.
2
Provision and Configure EspoCRM
Set up your EspoCRM instance either on your own Linux server or via a managed hosting provider. Recreate your organizational hierarchy, user roles, and security permissions within EspoCRM. Use the Administration panel to build custom entity fields and relationships that mirror your previous Salesforce configuration.
3
Map and Import Data
Format your exported Salesforce CSV files to align with EspoCRM import requirements. Leverage EspoCRM is built-in Import tool to systematically upload records, starting with parent objects like Accounts before moving to related Contacts and Opportunities. Run test imports on a staging environment first to verify that relational data links correctly.
4
Recreate Workflows and Integrations
Translate your Salesforce Process Builder flows, Workflow Rules, and Apex triggers into EspoCRM native workflow manager rules or formula scripts. Reconnect your essential third-party tools such as email clients, VoIP systems, and calendar sync. EspoCRM offers robust API endpoints and extension hooks to rebuild your automated business processes.
5
Train Your Team
Familiarize your sales and support teams with EspoCRM layout, which is often praised for being cleaner and faster than Salesforce Lightning. Conduct role-based training sessions focusing on daily tasks like pipeline management, email integration, and reporting. Provide cheat sheets highlighting where familiar Salesforce features reside in the new interface.
6
Execute the Cutover
Select a weekend or off-peak window to perform a delta data export from Salesforce to capture any records created since your initial test migration. Import these final updates into EspoCRM and perform a final system check. Revoke Salesforce access for your team, switch your DNS or bookmarks, and officially launch your new EspoCRM workspace.

⚠️ Common Challenges & How to Avoid Them

Complex Apex code and custom Salesforce Lightning components cannot be automatically transferred to EspoCRM.
Audit all custom code beforehand and rewrite critical business logic using EspoCRM built-in formula engine, workflow tools, or custom PHP extensions.
Mapping heavily customized Salesforce relational data structures to EspoCRM schema can lead to broken foreign keys.
Export relational data in a strict hierarchical order and utilize EspoCRM unique ID matching fields during the CSV import mapping stage.
Users accustomed to the vast Salesforce AppExchange ecosystem may experience feature shock with EspoCRM.
Explore the EspoCRM extension marketplace for pre-built integrations like VoIP and advanced maps, or budget for custom module development.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to migrate from Salesforce to EspoCRM?
EspoCRM core software is completely free and open-source under the AGPLv3 license. Your primary migration costs will involve server hosting, any premium EspoCRM extensions you choose to purchase, and potential contractor fees if you hire a developer to handle complex data mapping or custom workflow recreation.
Can I keep my historical Salesforce activity logs and emails in EspoCRM?
Yes, historical emails, tasks, and notes can be exported from Salesforce as CSV files and imported into EspoCRM activity streams. However, formatting date-time stamps and user ownership associations correctly requires careful data preparation before running the import tool.
Is EspoCRM difficult for former Salesforce users to learn?
Most users find EspoCRM much easier and faster to learn than Salesforce due to its streamlined, clutter-free user interface. While the terminology is very similar, the navigation requires fewer clicks, resulting in high user adoption rates right out of the box.

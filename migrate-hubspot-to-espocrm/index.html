How to Migrate from HubSpot to EspoCRM

💰 Save Free / $15-$800+/month/month 🔓 Own your data 📅 September 04, 2026

Migrating from HubSpot to EspoCRM is a strategic move for organizations looking to escape escalating subscription fees while retaining full ownership of their customer data. HubSpot is a powerhouse for inbound marketing and sales automation, but its pricing scales aggressively as contacts and user seats multiply, and advanced features are locked behind expensive tiers. EspoCRM offers a compelling open-source alternative that eliminates recurring SaaS overhead while providing a highly customizable, lightweight CRM framework.

Organizations typically make this switch when their HubSpot bills begin to outpace the value derived from proprietary tools, or when data privacy mandates require an on-premise deployment rather than cloud hosting. Because EspoCRM is self-hostable or available via affordable managed hosting, it grants complete control over database management and customization without arbitrary API limits. While HubSpot relies on a rigid, heavily integrated ecosystem, EspoCRM utilizes a modular architecture where you only activate the extensions you need, making it an ideal destination for teams seeking transparency, cost-efficiency, and operational independence.

🗺️ Migration Steps

1
Audit and Cleanse Your HubSpot Data
Before exporting, clean up your HubSpot contacts, companies, deals, and ticket properties to prevent migrating outdated records. Standardize custom properties and ensure that pipeline stages align with what you will configure in EspoCRM to streamline the mapping process.
2
Export Data from HubSpot
Navigate to your HubSpot settings and export your core objects—contacts, companies, deals, notes, and emails—as CSV files. Ensure that associated IDs like Company ID or Contact ID are included in the exports to maintain relational integrity during the import phase.
3
Set Up and Configure Your EspoCRM Instance
Deploy EspoCRM on your chosen server or cloud environment and configure your user accounts, roles, and security teams. Recreate your custom HubSpot properties, custom entities, and sales pipeline stages within EspoCRM Administration panel before touching any data files.
4
Import and Relate Data Files
Utilize EspoCRM built-in Data Import tool to upload your CSV files, starting with parent objects like Accounts and Contacts before importing dependent records like Deals and Tasks. Map your HubSpot column headers to the corresponding EspoCRM fields, taking advantage of unique identifiers to link related records properly.
5
Migrate Marketing Assets and Automations
Because HubSpot workflows and email templates do not export directly into EspoCRM, you must manually rebuild critical marketing campaigns and automation rules. Use EspoCRM internal workflow engine to replicate your lead assignment rules, task creation triggers, and email notifications.
6
Conduct Final Verification and Cutover
Run a parallel testing phase where a subset of your sales team validates contact histories, deal statuses, and email syncing in EspoCRM. Once verified, freeze HubSpot write access, perform a final delta import of recent records, and direct your team to make EspoCRM their primary CRM.

⚠️ Common Challenges & How to Avoid Them

Loss of native HubSpot marketing automation and form embeddings on external websites.
Rebuild lead capture forms using EspoCRM native web-to-lead generator and integrate alternative open-source marketing automation tools like Mautic for advanced email campaigns.
Complex relational data structures failing to link correctly during the CSV import process.
Import objects in strict chronological order—users, accounts, contacts, then deals—and use HubSpot unique record IDs as matching keys during the EspoCRM mapping stage.
User resistance when transitioning from HubSpot polished proprietary interface to EspoCRM minimalist layout.
Customize the EspoCRM layout using the built-in layout editor to match familiar workflows and conduct targeted training sessions focusing on EspoCRM quick search and global calendar features.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I automatically migrate my HubSpot email history and sales email tracking data to EspoCRM?
Direct migration of historical email threads via CSV is notoriously difficult due to formatting constraints. Instead, connect EspoCRM to your email provider using IMAP integration so ongoing communications are tracked, and manually archive critical historical email records if strictly necessary.
How does EspoCRM handle HubSpot custom properties and deeply nested objects?
EspoCRM allows you to easily create custom fields matching your HubSpot data types, including text, multi-enum, floats, and links. For deeply nested relational data, you may need to configure custom entity relationships in the EspoCRM developer panel before running your imports.
Is it possible to keep using HubSpot for marketing while using EspoCRM for sales?
Yes, many organizations adopt a hybrid approach by keeping HubSpot for inbound marketing forms and newsletters while using Zapier or custom webhooks to sync new leads directly into EspoCRM for the sales team to manage.

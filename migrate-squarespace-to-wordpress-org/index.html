📦 Migration Guide

How to Migrate from Squarespace to WordPress.org

💰 Save $16-$52/month/month 🔓 Own your data 📅 August 30, 2026

Migrating from Squarespace to WordPress.org is a pivotal move for site owners looking to escape rising subscription costs and rigid platform limitations. While Squarespace charges anywhere from sixteen to fifty-two dollars per month for locked-down, proprietary hosting and tiered feature gates, WordPress.org is free, open-source software that gives you total ownership of your codebase, database, and content. You might make this jump when your ecommerce store outgrows Squarespace's native inventory tools, when you need advanced database query capabilities, or simply when you want to eliminate recurring platform fees.

However, moving off Squarespace requires leaving a managed, all-in-one ecosystem for a self-hosted environment. Squarespace handles your SSL certificates, software updates, and server maintenance automatically, whereas WordPress.org requires you to select your own hosting provider, configure security layers, and manage plugins. This migration trades turnkey convenience for infinite customization, allowing you to install advanced SEO plugins, build custom post types, and access thousands of themes without platform-imposed restrictions.

🗺️ Migration Steps

1
Export Content from Squarespace
Log into your Squarespace dashboard, navigate to Settings, Advanced, and select Import/Export. Click Export to generate an XML file containing your blog posts, standard pages, text, and basic images. Note that Squarespace only permits a single blog page to be exported directly via XML, so additional blogs require manual handling.
2
Set Up Your WordPress.org Hosting and Core
Purchase a reliable web hosting plan and a domain name, then install the latest version of WordPress.org via your host's one-click installer. Choose a lightweight theme and essential plugins for security, SEO, and caching before initiating any content imports to ensure a clean foundation.
3
Import Content into WordPress
In your WordPress dashboard, go to Tools, Import, and install the WordPress importer plugin. Upload the XML file exported from Squarespace to populate your database with your old blog posts and pages. Review the imported text to ensure paragraph breaks and basic formatting translated correctly.
4
Rebuild Pages and Media Assets
Because Squarespace uses proprietary layout blocks that do not map to WordPress natively, you will need to manually rebuild your homepage and complex landing pages. Download and re-upload your images, as Squarespace image URLs do not always transfer cleanly during the XML import process.
5
Onboard Your Team and Configure Roles
Unlike Squarespace's simplified permission tiers, WordPress.org offers granular user roles ranging from Administrator to Subscriber. Invite your content editors and contributors to WordPress, assign appropriate capabilities, and run a quick training session on the Gutenberg editor.
6
Execute Domain Cutover and Redirects
Point your custom domain's DNS records from Squarespace to your new WordPress hosting provider by updating your nameservers or A records. Implement 301 redirects using a plugin like Redirection to ensure all old Squarespace URL paths route to your new WordPress pages without breaking inbound links.

⚠️ Common Challenges & How to Avoid Them

Squarespace blog blocks and custom page layouts do not transfer over through the standard XML export.
You will need to manually recreate complex layouts using the WordPress block editor or a page builder plugin after the initial import.
Images hosted on Squarespace may break or fail to download automatically during the XML import.
Use a third-party WordPress plugin designed to scrape and sideload external images into your local media library.
URL structures often change between Squarespace and WordPress, risking broken incoming links and lost SEO rankings.
Map out your old Squarespace URL slugs and set up explicit 301 redirects to the corresponding WordPress URLs before going live.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I automatically migrate my Squarespace online store products and customer data?
No, Squarespace does not export product variations, customer accounts, or order history via its standard XML tool. You will need to export your products as a CSV file and use a plugin like WooCommerce Product CSV Import to rebuild your store on WordPress.
How much does it cost to run a WordPress.org site compared to Squarespace?
While WordPress.org software is completely free, you must pay for web hosting, which typically ranges from three to twenty dollars per month. You may also incur costs for premium themes or plugins, though these are usually one-time purchases rather than recurring monthly platform fees.
Will my SEO rankings drop when I switch from Squarespace to WordPress.org?
If you properly configure 301 redirects for every page and maintain or improve your site loading speed, your SEO rankings should remain stable or improve over time. A temporary dip can occur if search engines encounter broken links or missing meta tags during the DNS cutover.

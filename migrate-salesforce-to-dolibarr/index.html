How to Migrate from Salesforce to Dolibarr

💰 Save $25-$300+/user/month/month 🔓 Own your data 📅 September 04, 2026

Migrating from Salesforce to Dolibarr is a strategic move for businesses looking to slash overhead while retaining robust ERP and CRM capabilities. Salesforce is a powerhouse, but its escalating per-user licensing fees ($25 to $300+ per user monthly), costly add-ons, and complex ecosystem can drain budgets for small to medium-sized enterprises. Dolibarr ERP/CRM offers a compelling open-source alternative. Because Dolibarr is free to download and self-host, companies eliminate recurring software subscriptions, gaining full control over their data infrastructure without sacrificing essential features like invoicing, third-party management, and pipeline tracking.

Organizations typically make the switch when they outgrow the financial justification for Salesforce's enterprise tiers or when they require an on-premise deployment for strict data sovereignty. While Salesforce excels in massive, highly customized multi-departmental architectures, Dolibarr shines for lean operations that need a straightforward, modular platform without the bloat. Transitioning requires careful planning—mapping Salesforce objects like Accounts, Contacts, and Opportunities to Dolibarr's database structure—but the long-term cost savings and absence of vendor lock-in make the migration effort well worth it for budget-conscious teams.

🗺️ Migration Steps

1
Audit and Cleanse Salesforce Data
Before exporting, review your Salesforce org to archive inactive accounts and standardize fields. Standardize picklist values in Salesforce so they align cleanly with Dolibarr third-party and contact categories, preventing data corruption during the transfer.
2
Export Data from Salesforce
Use Salesforce Data Loader or standard CSV export reports to extract your core relational data. You will need separate CSV files for Accounts, Contacts, Products, Suppliers, and Open Opportunities to match Dolibarr's relational import modules.
3
Install and Configure Dolibarr
Deploy Dolibarr on your chosen server environment, whether local or cloud-hosted. Enable the specific modules you need—such as Third Parties, Commercial (for proposals and orders), and Invoice modules—to mirror the functionality you utilized in Salesforce.
4
Map and Import CSV Data
Utilize Dolibarr's built-in Admin Tools data import assistant to upload your CSV files. Carefully map Salesforce fields like Billing Street and Account Name to Dolibarr's corresponding database attributes, running test imports on a staging environment first.
5
Configure Users and Permissions
Recreate your sales and administrative user accounts within Dolibarr. Assign user groups and permission profiles that reflect your previous Salesforce security matrix, ensuring staff only access the modules relevant to their roles.
6
Run Parallel Testing and Cutover
Run Salesforce and Dolibarr concurrently for one billing cycle to verify that invoicing, quote generation, and CRM logs match expectations. Once verified, freeze Salesforce writes, perform a final delta import of recent leads, and officially cut over your team to Dolibarr.

⚠️ Common Challenges & How to Avoid Them

Salesforce uses complex relational objects like Leads, Accounts, and Contacts separated cleanly, whereas Dolibarr combines companies and people into 'Third Parties' with distinct contact links.
Restructure your exported CSV data to merge company and individual attributes into Dolibarr's third-party and linked-contact format before running the import tool.
Salesforce automation rules, Apex triggers, and Process Builder workflows have no direct equivalent in base Dolibarr.
Audit your critical workflows and replace them using Dolibarr's built-in notification triggers, hooks, or community modules from the Dolistore marketplace.
Self-hosting Dolibarr means your IT team is now responsible for database backups, security patches, and server uptime instead of Salesforce's managed cloud.
Set up automated nightly database dumps and server monitoring scripts, or contract a managed hosting provider specializing in PHP/MySQL applications.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I migrate my historical Salesforce email logs and activity history to Dolibarr?
Direct migration of historical Salesforce activity timelines is difficult due to proprietary data schemas. Most organizations choose to export past emails and notes as static PDF or CSV archive files and attach them to the relevant third-party records in Dolibarr for reference.
How does Dolibarr handle custom fields that we created in Salesforce?
Dolibarr features a robust Extrafields management system accessible via the setup menu. You can recreate your custom Salesforce text, date, and select fields in Dolibarr before importing your CSV data.
Is there any official migration tool provided by Dolibarr to import from Salesforce?
There is no automated one-click Salesforce-to-Dolibarr migration wizard. Migration relies on standardizing your Salesforce data into CSV formats and utilizing Dolibarr's native mass import assistant tool.

📦 Migration Guide

How to Migrate from Typeform to Formbricks

💰 Save Free / $25-$83/month/month 🔓 Own your data 📅 August 28, 2026

Migrating from Typeform to Formbricks is a strategic move for product and growth teams looking to transition from expensive proprietary software to a flexible, open-source survey stack. While Typeform is renowned for its polished conversational UI and aesthetic appeal, its pricing scales rapidly—ranging from $25 to $83 per month—and data residency is strictly bound to their proprietary cloud. Formbricks offers a compelling alternative by combining a sleek, modern interface with the freedom of open-source software, allowing teams to either use their managed cloud or self-host to keep sensitive user data strictly within their own infrastructure.

Teams typically initiate this migration when their Typeform response volumes outgrow lower-tier pricing limits or when privacy regulations demand complete data ownership. Formbricks excels at in-product surveys triggered by user behavior, making it a natural fit for SaaS companies that want to capture micro-feedback inside their application rather than relying solely on standalone link shares. By moving to Formbricks, you eliminate recurring per-response licensing overhead while gaining deep integration capabilities directly tied to your product analytics and user lifecycles.

🗺️ Migration Steps

1
Audit and Export Your Typeform Surveys
Begin by reviewing your active Typeform workspace to identify which forms need to be migrated and which can be archived. Export your historical response data as CSV files from the Typeform dashboard to ensure you retain a permanent archive of past submissions.
2
Initialize Your Formbricks Environment
Choose between deploying Formbricks Cloud for instant access or self-hosting the open-source repository via Docker for complete data sovereignty. Configure your organization workspace, invite team members, and set up your primary product environment parameters.
3
Rebuild Your Forms in Formbricks
Use the Formbricks visual builder to recreate your Typeform questionnaires, leveraging question types like multi-choice, ratings, and open text. Because Formbricks is optimized for in-product micro-surveys, you can design highly targeted flows that feel native to your application UI.
4
Migrate Historical Data
Since Formbricks does not feature a direct one-click Typeform data importer, use the CSV exports you generated earlier to migrate essential historical records into your data warehouse or CRM. For active survey continuity, inform ongoing respondents that you have updated your feedback infrastructure.
5
Install the Formbricks SDK
Replace the Typeform embed scripts or link buttons on your website and app with the lightweight Formbricks JavaScript SDK. This enables advanced user identification and event-based triggers that allow surveys to appear based on precise user actions rather than static page loads.
6
Test Triggers and Cut Over
Run comprehensive end-to-end testing in a staging environment to verify that survey responses flow correctly into your Formbricks dashboard. Once verified, deactivate your live Typeform links and complete the official transition to your new Formbricks setup.

⚠️ Common Challenges & How to Avoid Them

Typeform embeds complex logic jumps that do not have an exact 1:1 equivalent in Formbricks logic builder.
Simplify your survey branching paths during the rebuild process and leverage Formbricks' conditional display logic to achieve the desired user flow.
Historical response data cannot be automatically imported back into the Formbricks user interface for unified analytics.
Store your Typeform CSV exports in your internal data warehouse or BI tool to maintain historical reporting continuity alongside new Formbricks data.
Self-hosting Formbricks requires ongoing server maintenance, environment variable management, and database backups.
Utilize Formbricks Cloud if your team prefers a fully managed SaaS experience without the overhead of infrastructure administration.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I automatically import my Typeform questions and responses directly into Formbricks?
No, Formbricks does not currently support direct automated migration of active Typeform questionnaires or historical response databases. You will need to manually recreate your forms using the Formbricks builder and export your past data as CSV files for archiving.
How does Formbricks pricing compare to Typeform?
Typeform charges a monthly subscription fee ranging from $25 to $83+ that scales based on response limits and advanced features. Formbricks offers a generous free tier, affordable cloud options, and the ability to self-host the open-source software completely free of licensing costs.
Is Formbricks better suited for website links or in-app surveys?
While Formbricks can be shared via standalone URLs much like Typeform, it is specifically optimized for in-product micro-surveys. The Formbricks SDK allows you to trigger surveys based on user behavior, attributes, and events directly inside your web application.

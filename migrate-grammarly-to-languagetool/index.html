🗺️ Migration Steps

1
Export and Audit Personal Dictionaries
Grammarly does not provide a direct one-click export button for your personal dictionary or custom snippets. You will need to manually copy specialized terms, acronyms, and names from your Grammarly account settings so you can re-enter them into LanguageTool.
2
Deactivate and Remove Grammarly Extensions
Before installing the new software, completely uninstall Grammarly browser extensions, desktop apps, and Microsoft Office add-ins. Leaving both tools active simultaneously causes cursor jumping, conflicting underlines, and browser performance slowdowns.
3
Install LanguageTool Extensions and Apps
Download the official LanguageTool extensions for Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge, and install the desktop app for macOS or Windows. Log into your new LanguageTool account to sync your preferences and activate premium features if you upgraded from the free tier.
4
Rebuild Custom Rules and Dictionary
Navigate to your LanguageTool account dashboard to input the vocabulary list you previously salvaged from Grammarly. Take advantage of LanguageTool's advanced style guide features to set up custom rules that match your company's specific tone and formatting guidelines.
5
Conduct Team Onboarding and Policy Updates
If you are migrating an entire team, update your internal documentation to remove references to Grammarly. Host a brief training session to familiarize staff with LanguageTool's keyboard shortcuts, sidebar interface, and multilingual checking capabilities.
6
Perform Final Cutover and Monitor Adoption
Cancel your auto-renewing Grammarly subscription to prevent double-billing after your current cycle ends. Monitor team feedback over the first two weeks to address any workflow friction and ensure users are fully utilizing LanguageTool.

Notion vs AppFlowy (2026)

Overview

Notion is a cloud‑based all‑in‑one workspace for notes, databases, and project management, while AppFlowy is an open‑source, self‑hosted alternative that provides similar building blocks with full data ownership. This comparison helps knowledge workers, product teams, and small‑to‑medium enterprises decide whether the proprietary convenience of Notion or the free, self‑hosted flexibility of AppFlowy better fits their workflow. Switch if you need lower cost and control over where your data lives.

Key Differences

  • Cost: Notion charges $8‑$20 per user per month; AppFlowy is free (only server hosting costs).
  • Data ownership: Notion stores data on its SaaS platform; AppFlowy stores data in your own database, giving you full ownership.
  • Setup complexity: Notion requires no installation; AppFlowy needs Docker/VM setup and occasional maintenance.
  • Scalability: Notion scales automatically in the cloud; AppFlowy scaling depends on your infrastructure and may require manual load‑balancing.
  • Feature/Integration: Notion includes native integrations with Slack, Google Drive, and Zapier; AppFlowy currently offers only community‑built plugins and lacks official Zapier support.

Pros and Cons

Notion

Pros
  • Intuitive drag‑and‑drop UI with rich content blocks.
  • Robust native integrations (Slack, Google Workspace, Zapier).
  • Automatic real‑time collaboration and version history.
  • Scales instantly without infrastructure management.

Cons

  • Subscription cost grows linearly with headcount.
  • Data is locked to Notion’s cloud; export is limited to Markdown/HTML.
  • Limited offline capabilities.
  • No self‑hosting option for compliance‑heavy industries.

AppFlowy

Pros
  • Completely free and open‑source; no per‑user fees.
  • Full data ownership; you host the database yourself.
  • Highly customizable UI via community plugins.
  • Can be run on low‑cost VPS or on‑premises hardware.

Cons

  • Requires technical expertise to install, update, and back up.
  • Fewer built‑in integrations; no official Zapier connector.
  • Collaboration features (real‑time cursors, comments) are still maturing.
  • Limited mobile app polish compared with Notion.

When to Choose Each

Notion is ideal for fast‑moving teams of 5‑200 members who prioritize out‑of‑the‑box collaboration, deep integrations, and zero‑maintenance infrastructure. If your organization values convenience over cost and does not have strict data‑sovereignty requirements, Notion delivers a polished experience with minimal IT overhead.

AppFlowy fits teams of 10‑500 users that need to keep data on‑premises or in a private cloud, have a modest budget, and possess at least one technically capable member to manage the server. It shines for organizations with compliance mandates, developer‑centric cultures, or those looking to avoid recurring SaaS fees while still needing a flexible workspace.

Migration Path

  1. Export from Notion: In Notion, go to Settings → Export, choose “Markdown & CSV”, and download the ZIP file.
  2. Prepare the server: Deploy AppFlowy via Docker (docker run -d -p 8000:8000 appflowyio/appflowy) or on a VPS following the official docs.
  3. Import content: In AppFlowy, open the Import Wizard, select the exported Markdown files, and map them to pages or tables as needed.
  4. Recreate databases: Convert Notion CSV tables to AppFlowy’s relational tables using the built‑in CSV import tool.
  5. Validate and train: Verify that all pages render correctly, adjust permissions, and run a short onboarding session for users.

Our Take

AppFlowy has reached a production‑ready state for most small‑to‑medium teams that can allocate a few hours of engineering time for setup and occasional maintenance. Its core note‑taking, kanban, and relational table features are stable, and the community actively contributes plugins for common workflows. For organizations that need zero‑cost licensing and full control over data residency, AppFlowy offers a compelling alternative to Notion’s subscription model.

However, a notable technical limitation is the lack of mature real‑time collaboration. While basic multi‑user editing works, features like live cursor presence, granular comment threads, and instant conflict resolution are still in beta and may not meet the expectations of large, fast‑paced product teams. Teams that rely heavily on simultaneous editing or extensive Zapier automations should weigh this shortcoming carefully.

Recommendation: Switch to AppFlowy if you have ≤ 500 users, a compliance or cost‑driven need for self‑hosting, and can live without full‑fledged real‑time editing or official Zapier integration. Stick with Notion if you need polished mobile apps, robust native integrations, or a truly frictionless collaborative experience out of the box.

Who Should Switch

  • Startups (5‑30 members) that need to keep product roadmaps in‑house and want to eliminate SaaS spend.
  • Compliance‑focused teams (20‑100 members) such as legal or healthcare that require data to reside on a private server.
  • Developer‑centric groups (10‑50 members) that want to customize UI components via open‑source plugins.
  • Educational institutions (30‑200 users) looking for a free, self‑hosted workspace for labs and projects.
  • Remote NGOs (15‑80 members) that lack reliable internet for constant cloud sync and can host a low‑cost VPS.

FAQ

How do I export my Notion workspace for AppFlowy?

Use Notion’s built‑in export tool (Settings → Export) and choose “Markdown & CSV”. The export is a ZIP file containing .md files for pages and .csv files for databases, which AppFlowy can import directly.

Does AppFlowy have a mobile app?

AppFlowy offers a community‑maintained Android app and an iOS web‑view wrapper, but they lack some of the polish and offline sync capabilities found in Notion’s native mobile apps.

Can I integrate AppFlowy with Slack or Zapier?

Official Slack and Zapier integrations are not available yet. You can build custom webhooks or use community plugins, but they require manual setup and may not be as seamless as Notion’s native connections.

Is real‑time collaborative editing reliable in AppFlowy?

Real‑time editing works for basic scenarios, but features like live cursors and instant conflict resolution are still in beta. Expect occasional sync delays compared with Notion’s mature collaboration engine.

Will I need to pay for hosting AppFlowy?

AppFlowy itself is free, but you must cover the cost of the server or VPS where it runs. For a modest 2‑CPU, 4 GB VPS, typical monthly hosting fees range from $5 to $15.

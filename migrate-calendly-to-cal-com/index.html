📦 Migration Guide

How to Migrate from Calendly to Cal.com

💰 Save Free / $8-$16/user/month/month 🔓 Own your data 📅 August 26, 2026

Migrating from Calendly to Cal.com is a logical step for individuals and teams looking to escape per-user SaaS pricing in favor of an open-source, highly extensible scheduling infrastructure. While Calendly popularized frictionless cloud-based appointment booking, its proprietary ecosystem limits deep customization, white-labeling, and data ownership. Cal.com offers a robust alternative that mirrors Calendly core event types, round-robin routing, and calendar integrations while giving you the freedom to self-host or leverage their managed cloud platform.

You should consider making the switch when your team outgrows Calendly tier-based pricing limits, requires custom branding without rigid enterprise paywalls, or needs to integrate scheduling workflows directly into custom application databases via webhooks and APIs. Cal.com provides an intuitive routing form builder and infrastructure that scales cost-effectively, making it the premier choice for developer-centric organizations and growing businesses wanting absolute control over their meeting workflows.

🗺️ Migration Steps

1
Audit and Export Calendly Settings
Begin by reviewing your active Calendly event types, active team members, and webhook configurations. While Calendly does not provide a direct one-click data migration tool to export event types, you can manually document your availability schedules, buffer times, and custom intake questions to prepare for recreation in Cal.com.
2
Set Up Your Cal.com Account and Connect Calendars
Sign up for a Cal.com account or spin up a self-hosted instance using Docker if you require data sovereignty. Connect your primary Google Calendar, Outlook, or Apple accounts just as you did in Calendly to ensure conflict detection syncs instantly across all your personal and professional calendars.
3
Recreate Event Types and Routing Forms
Use Cal.com interface to rebuild your standard 15-minute, 30-minute, and custom event types. Take advantage of Cal.com advanced features by setting up granular team routing forms that dynamically qualify leads and assign meetings based on specific visitor inputs.
4
Onboard Team Members and Roles
Invite your colleagues to your Cal.com organization dashboard and assign appropriate administrative or member permissions. Unlike Calendly billing model which charges strictly per active user seat, Cal.com open-source model or cloud tiers offer flexible scaling for growing organizations.
5
Update Embeds and Share New Links
Replace your old Calendly inline embeds and popup widget codes on your website with Cal.com corresponding React components or iframe snippets. Update your email signatures, social media bios, and outbound sales sequences to point directly to your new Cal.com booking URLs.
6
Decommission Calendly and Test End-to-End
Run a series of test bookings through your new Cal.com links to verify that calendar invites, SMS reminders, and video conferencing links like Zoom or Google Meet generate correctly. Once verified, cancel your Calendly subscription to complete the migration.

⚠️ Common Challenges & How to Avoid Them

Calendly does not offer an automated export button for event types and historical booking data.
You will need to manually copy event descriptions, questions, and availability rules. Plan this transition during a scheduled maintenance window to avoid booking disruptions.
Existing marketing materials, email templates, and website embeds still point to your old Calendly URLs.
Use URL forwarding on your custom domain if applicable, or systematically update your high-traffic touchpoints using a centralized link management tool before canceling Calendly.
Team members accustomed to Calendly simplified interface may experience a brief learning curve with Cal.com advanced customization options.
Host a short internal training session highlighting Cal.com routing forms and app store integrations to help your team feel comfortable with the new dashboard.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I keep my existing calendar integrations when moving from Calendly to Cal.com?
Yes. Cal.com supports seamless synchronization with Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook, Apple Calendar, and Exchange. Your availability rules and conflict checking will function identically to your previous Calendly setup.
Is Cal.com really free compared to Calendly paid tiers?
Cal.com offers a robust free tier for individuals and a completely free open-source code base that you can self-host on your own infrastructure. They also offer paid managed cloud plans for teams needing advanced features and enterprise support without managing servers.
How do I handle custom booking links and domains during the switch?
Cal.com allows you to set custom usernames and even white-label your scheduling links with custom domains on higher tiers. This ensures your branding remains consistent for clients clicking your new booking URLs.

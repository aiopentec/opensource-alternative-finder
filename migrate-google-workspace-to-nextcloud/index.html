📦 Migration Guide

How to Migrate from Google Workspace to Nextcloud

💰 Save $6-$18/user/month/month 🔓 Own your data 🖥️ Setup: Moderate (~1-2 hours) 📅 August 27, 2026

Migrating from Google Workspace to Nextcloud offers organizations a powerful path toward data sovereignty, privacy compliance, and substantial cost reduction. While Workspace locks businesses into subscription tiers ranging from $6 to $18 per user per month with data residing in Google-managed cloud infrastructure, Nextcloud is open-source and can be hosted on-premises or on private cloud servers. Organizations typically make this leap when strict regulatory requirements mandate absolute control over corporate files, or when scaling user counts makes per-seat Google pricing economically unsustainable.

Moving away from Google Drive, Docs, and Gmail to Nextcloud means trading a proprietary ecosystem for a modular platform where your team manages the underlying storage. However, this transition requires careful planning. You are moving from a globally distributed SaaS giant to self-managed or dedicated hosting infrastructure. Success hinges on a structured approach that accounts for file structures, user authentication, and organizational change management.

🗺️ Migration Steps

1
Prepare and Provision Your Nextcloud Environment
Before touching your Google data, deploy Nextcloud on your own Linux server or through a trusted hosting provider. Ensure you allocate sufficient storage capacity to match your current Google Drive usage and configure HTTPS with a valid SSL certificate for secure client connections.
2
Export Google Drive Data via Takeout
Use Google Takeout to export your organization's files, shared drives, and user data archives. This tool compiles your entire Google Workspace repository into standardized formats like zip archives containing Office documents, PDFs, and native Google Docs converted into Microsoft Office formats.
3
Configure User Accounts and Authentication
Recreate your user base in Nextcloud or integrate the platform with your existing Identity Provider using LDAP or SAML single sign-on. Matching your Google Workspace directory structure ensures a seamless login experience for your team on day one.
4
Import Files and Folder Hierarchies
Upload the extracted Google data into Nextcloud using the Nextcloud command-line interface via the occ files:scan command for massive repositories, or use WebDAV for smaller batches. This preserves your directory trees and ensures metadata is correctly indexed by the Nextcloud database.
5
Onboard Team Members and Client Apps
Distribute Nextcloud desktop sync clients and mobile apps to your staff, replacing the Google Drive desktop application. Provide training on the Nextcloud web interface, file versioning, and collaborative editing tools like Collabora Online or OnlyOffice.
6
Execute Final Cutover and Decommission Workspace
Perform a final delta sync of any files modified during the migration window, switch your DNS or domain routing if necessary, and officially deprecate your Google Workspace active licenses once your team is fully operational on Nextcloud.

⚠️ Common Challenges & How to Avoid Them

Native Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides formats do not automatically convert into editable Nextcloud files during export.
Google Takeout automatically converts native Google formats into Microsoft Office equivalents (.docx, .xlsx, .pptx). Install Collabora Online or OnlyOffice on your Nextcloud server so users can continue editing these files directly in their browsers.
Self-hosting Nextcloud lacks the infinite, hands-off scalability and automatic redundancy of Google Workspace's cloud infrastructure.
Implement a robust backup strategy utilizing external storage targets or object storage providers like AWS S3 or Backblaze B2, alongside automated database snapshots.
Users accustomed to Google Drive's search speed and UI may experience friction adapting to Nextcloud.
Enable the Nextcloud Full Text Search app powered by Elasticsearch to deliver fast, deep file indexing that rivals Google's search capabilities.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How are native Google Docs handled during a migration to Nextcloud?
Google Takeout exports native documents as Microsoft Office files. Once uploaded to Nextcloud, you can view and edit them seamlessly by integrating an office suite like Collabora or OnlyOffice directly into your Nextcloud instance.
Can I keep my existing email and calendar in Google Workspace while moving files to Nextcloud?
Yes, a hybrid approach is entirely possible. Many organizations migrate file storage to Nextcloud to save on costs and improve privacy while keeping Gmail and Google Calendar active, using Nextcloud solely as a Google Drive replacement.
What are the ongoing costs of running Nextcloud compared to Google Workspace?
While Nextcloud software is free and open-source, your costs will shift to server hosting, storage infrastructure, and optional enterprise support subscriptions if you purchase Nextcloud Enterprise. For larger teams, this typically results in massive savings compared to per-user Google Workspace fees.

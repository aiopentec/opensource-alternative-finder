📦 Migration Guide

How to Migrate from Canva to Penpot

💰 Save Free / $15-$30/user/month/month 🔓 Own your data 🖥️ Setup: Easy (~30 mins) 📅 August 25, 2026

Migrating from Canva to Penpot represents a strategic shift from a template-driven marketing platform to a professional, standards-based design and prototyping tool. While Canva excels at quick social media graphics and presentations using pre-made assets, design teams often outgrow its rigid layout constraints, proprietary ecosystem, and lack of true vector manipulation. Penpot offers a compelling open-source alternative that bridges the gap between designers and developers by utilizing web standards like SVG and CSS Flexbox natively. Organizations make this move when they need precise control over UI design, component systems, and scalable design tokens without paying escalating per-user subscription fees.

Transitioning requires a shift in workflow philosophy. Canva users are accustomed to relying on a massive library of stock elements, automated animations, and drag-and-drop page layouts. Penpot, by contrast, functions much like Figma or traditional vector editors, empowering you to build custom design systems from scratch using components, overrides, and responsive layouts. By migrating, your team gains complete data ownership, local-first deployment options via self-hosting, and a robust environment for building actual digital products rather than just static marketing collateral.

🗺️ Migration Steps

1
Audit and Export Existing Canva Assets
Review your Canva workspace to identify essential brand assets, reusable graphics, and active design files that need to be migrated. Export static layouts and multi-page presentations as PDFs or high-resolution PNGs for archival reference, and download individual vector illustrations as SVGs whenever possible since Canva permits this format.
2
Set Up Your Penpot Workspace and Team
Create your organization account on Penpot, either by utilizing their managed cloud service or by deploying a self-hosted instance on your own infrastructure. Invite your design and development stakeholders, then establish your workspace teams and permission structures to match your operational workflow.
3
Rebuild Brand Guidelines and Design Tokens
Since direct file conversion from Canva to Penpot is not natively supported due to differing underlying formats, you must recreate your brand foundations. Input your official color palettes, typography hierarchies, and logo assets directly into Penpot libraries as reusable styles and design tokens.
4
Recreate Templates and Layout Components
Take your most frequently used Canva templates, such as social media carousels or pitch decks, and rebuild them in Penpot using modern layout engines. Leverage Penpot grid systems, constraints, and components to make these new templates responsive and far more flexible than their Canva predecessors.
5
Onboard Your Team to Vector and Grid Workflows
Conduct internal training sessions to help former Canva users adapt to Penpot interface mechanics, such as boolean operations, precise vector paths, and CSS-based layout logic. Emphasize how Penpot component overrides work differently than Canva grouped elements to ensure a smooth transition.
6
Execute the Final Cutover
Establish a firm deadline to stop creating new collateral inside Canva and officially shift all active projects into Penpot. Archive your old Canva workspace to preserve historical records, and direct all team members to use Penpot as the single source of truth for design work.

⚠️ Common Challenges & How to Avoid Them

Canva designs cannot be directly imported into Penpot as editable vector layers.
Accept that layouts must be manually rebuilt. Use exported images as visual references side-by-side in Penpot while you reconstruct the graphics cleanly using vector tools and components.
Penpot lacks Canva extensive built-in library of stock photos, audio tracks, and automated video templates.
Curate an external organizational asset library using open-source stock imagery providers and integrate third-party digital asset management tools to supply your team with raw media.
Team members accustomed to Canva drag-and-drop simplicity may struggle with Penpot professional component and constraints logic.
Invest time in structured internal workshops focusing on CSS Grid, Flexbox concepts, and design system scaling to bridge the knowledge gap rapidly.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I automatically convert my Canva files into editable Penpot files?
No, direct file conversion is not possible because Canva uses a proprietary closed format while Penpot relies on open standards like SVG. You will need to manually recreate your active designs and templates inside Penpot.
Is Penpot truly free to use compared to Canva paid subscriptions?
Yes, Penpot is completely open-source and free to use on their cloud platform, and you can also self-host it at no cost. This removes the escalating per-user monthly fees associated with Canva Team and Enterprise accounts.
How does Penpot handle developer handoff differently than Canva?
Penpot is built specifically with developers in mind, offering native inspection tools that output production-ready CSS code based on web standards like Flexbox. Canva primarily offers basic export options and simple sharing links that lack precise layout code inspection.
